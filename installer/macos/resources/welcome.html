Vibe Plugin for Premiere Pro

Search YouTube or paste a link, pick a quality, and the file lands in your project as Premiere-ready H.264.

This installs the panel for every user on this Mac, and turns on unsigned panels for your account — Premiere needs that to load any open-source panel.

Please quit Premiere Pro before you continue.

macOS support is experimental. Conversion, cutting and MP3/WAV need an ffmpeg binary in ~/Library/Application Support/VibePlugin/bin/.
