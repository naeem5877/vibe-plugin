It does **not bundle** the programs below. On first use the panel downloads them from their official release pages into `%APPDATA%\VibePlugin\bin` (Windows) or `~/Library/Application Support/VibePlugin/bin` (macOS). They are separate programs under their own licenses, started as child processes.

| Program | Purpose | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| yt-dlp | Extracts and downloads media, writes subtitles | The Unlicense | https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp |
| QuickJS-ng | Small JS engine used to solve YouTube's challenges | MIT | https://github.com/quickjs-ng/quickjs |
| FFmpeg (yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds, GPL build) | Merging, converting, cutting | GPL v3 (this build) | https://github.com/yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds |
